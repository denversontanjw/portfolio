ACRA · 2026
Public Accountant Application Portal
End-to-end digital transformation of Singapore's Public Accountant registration process — replacing a highly manual, multi-week application process with a streamlined eService platform, cutting pre-eligibility assessment from 4 hours to 30 minutes per application.
300 Man-Hours Saved · ~80 PA/yr
Problem
Processing each PA application required 4 hours of manual pre-eligibility checks, averaged 15 days end-to-end, and staff handled 60–80 applications annually entirely through manual steps — a significant bottleneck on ACRA's regulatory capacity.
Approach
Streamlined the entire application journey — from guided eService submission through automated eligibility screening to officer assessment and approval — integrating regulatory data sources to eliminate manual lookups and compressing turnaround time at every stage.
